# small regressions cannot walk it downhill. Each floor below is what the fake model measured
# on 2026-11-05 over tests/corpora and tests/fixtures/evals, when the gate was built. Raising
# one is a reviewed change; lowering one is a decision.
#
#   keep <metric> <= | >= <limit>             an absolute limit
#   keep <metric> <= | >= <+/-pct>% of <floor> a limit relative to the floor
#
# Numbers are non-negative integers or fractions n/d. A share keeps its counts: 19/30 is 19 of
# the 30 benign items proposed from.
